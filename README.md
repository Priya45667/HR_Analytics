

# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI
This dashboard was developed as part of my learning journey in Data Analytics to understand employee attrition patterns and HR performance metrics using Power BI.


##  Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** and **Microsoft Excel**. The primary objective of this project is to analyze employee data, identify patterns related to employee attrition, and provide meaningful insights that can support HR teams in making data-driven decisions.

Employee attrition is one of the biggest challenges for any organization because high employee turnover increases recruitment costs, reduces productivity, and affects overall business performance. This dashboard helps HR professionals understand employee behavior by analyzing workforce demographics, department-wise attrition, education background, age distribution, and job satisfaction.

The dashboard presents HR data in a visually interactive format, allowing users to quickly identify trends and areas that require attention. It provides a complete overview of employee statistics and helps management improve employee retention strategies.



#  Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Analyze employee attrition across the organization.
- Monitor key HR performance indicators (KPIs).
- Identify departments with higher employee turnover.
- Understand workforce demographics.
- Analyze attrition based on education field, age group, and gender.
- Support HR decision-making through interactive dashboards.

#  Dashboard Insights

### Overall Workforce

The organization consists of **1470 employees**, out of which **1233 employees are currently active**, while **237 employees have left the organization**, resulting in an overall **attrition rate of 16.12%**.

### Department-wise Attrition

The **Research & Development (R&D)** department recorded the highest employee attrition among all departments, followed by the Sales department. This indicates that employee retention strategies should focus more on these departments.


### Employee Distribution by Age

The majority of employees belong to the **25–34 years** age group, making it the largest workforce segment within the organization.


### Education Field Analysis

Employees from the **Life Sciences** education background experienced the highest employee attrition compared to other education fields.


### Job Satisfaction

The dashboard also compares job satisfaction ratings across different job roles, helping HR teams identify roles where employee satisfaction may require improvement.


### Gender-wise Attrition

The dashboard provides gender-based attrition analysis across different age groups, helping HR teams understand workforce behavior more effectively.
